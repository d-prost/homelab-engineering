# Code of conduct

Keep discussion technical, respectful and useful.

This repository is about engineering decisions and lessons from a real self-hosted environment. Critique the design, evidence or reasoning rather than the person behind it.

Do not post credentials, private addressing, internal DNS names, recovery artifacts or other operational details that could weaken somebody's environment. If a security issue involves this repository, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.

Harassment, personal attacks and deliberately disruptive behavior are not welcome. Repeated or serious problems may be removed or blocked at the maintainer's discretion.
